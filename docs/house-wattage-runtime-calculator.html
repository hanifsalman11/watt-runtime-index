Frequently Asked Questions (House)

How many watts does a House use per hour?

While active, a House consumes 350 watts (2.92 Amps at 120V). With a typical 65% duty cycle, it uses approximately 227.5 watt-hours (Wh) per clock hour.

Can a 1000Wh portable power station run a House?

A 1000Wh LiFePO4 power station (delivering ~850Wh of usable AC power after inverter conversion) will run a House for approximately 3.7 hours, provided its inverter surge rating exceeds 900W.

What size solar panel do I need to run a House off-grid?

To offset the 227.5Wh hourly consumption during daytime operation while recharging your battery bank, pair your power station with at least a 400W Portable Folding Monocrystalline Solar Panel.
